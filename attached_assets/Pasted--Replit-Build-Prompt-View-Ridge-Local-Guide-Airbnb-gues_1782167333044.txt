## 9 — Practical / Good to Know

- **Urgent care & ER:** UW Medical Center and Northwest Hospital are both close; **Seattle Children's** is right in the neighborhood.
- **Pharmacy:** the **Safeway pharmacy** (same store as the grocery run above) is the nearest. Note that Seattle no longer has a true 24-hour pharmacy, so plan prescription pickups during normal hours.

## 11 — Skiing & Winter Sports *(I love to ski, so this one's close to my heart)*

Some of the best skiing in the country sits within day-trip range of the house. Roughly in order of distance:

- **The Summit at Snoqualmie (incl. Alpental)** — the closest, about an hour east on I-90. Four areas (Summit West, Central, and East, plus **Alpental**) cover everyone: mellow, family-friendly groomers and **night skiing** at the Summit areas, and serious steep terrain at Alpental. Ideal for a half-day or after-lunch laps. (Ikon Pass.) *(Skiing · Day trip)*
- **Stevens Pass** — about 1.5–2 hrs north on US-2. A bigger-mountain feel with great variety and legendary tree skiing. (Epic Pass.) *(Skiing · Day trip)*
- **Crystal Mountain** — about 1.5–2 hrs southeast, right by Mt Rainier. Washington's largest ski area, with the highest lift-served terrain in the state and a gondola with jaw-dropping Rainier views. (Ikon Pass.) *(Skiing · Day trip)*
- **Mt Baker** — about 2.5–3 hrs north. A no-frills, independent powder mecca that famously holds the world record for the most snowfall in a single season. Worth the drive on a big powder day. *(Skiing · Day trip)*

**Gear — buy or rent**
- **evo** — the local action-sports institution; flagship in **Fremont** (3500 Stone Way N) with a full ski/snowboard shop, rentals, boot fitting, and tuning. They also run a satellite rental shop **right at Snoqualmie Pass** (across from Summit West) if you'd rather grab gear on the way up. *(Skiing · Shopping)*
- **REI Flagship** — the original, iconic **REI** is right here in Seattle (222 Yale Ave N, South Lake Union). Beyond ski/snowboard gear, rentals, and shop services, the store is a Seattle attraction in its own right — an 80,000-sq-ft "timbered cathedral" with a 65-foot indoor climbing pinnacle, an outdoor bike-test trail, and a café. Worth a stop even in summer. *(Skiing · Shopping · Sights · kid-friendly)*
